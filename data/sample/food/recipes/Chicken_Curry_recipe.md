# Chicken Curry

*Fictional sample recipe — see README.md.*

Prep: 20 min · Cook: 40 min · Serves 4

## Ingredients
- 500g chicken breast, diced
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 tbsp mild curry paste
- 400ml coconut milk
- 200g rice, to serve

## Method
1. Fry the onion until soft, add the curry paste and cook for 1 minute.
2. Add the chicken and brown on all sides.
3. Pour in the coconut milk, simmer for 25 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
4. Serve over rice.

## Notes
- Keep the curry paste mild for the kids — serve extra chilli on the side for adults.
