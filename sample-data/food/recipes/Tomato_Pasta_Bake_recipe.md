# Tomato Pasta Bake

*Fictional sample recipe — see README.md.*

Quelle: [Example Site](https://example.com/recipes/tomato-pasta-bake)

Prep: 15 min · Cook: 30 min · Serves 4

## Ingredients
- 400g pasta (penne or fusilli)
- 2 x 400g tins chopped tomatoes
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 150g mozzarella, grated
- Handful fresh basil
- Salt and pepper

## Method
1. Cook the pasta according to packet instructions, drain.
2. Fry the onion and garlic until soft, add the tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes.
3. Stir the pasta into the sauce, transfer to an ovenproof dish, top with mozzarella.
4. Bake at 200°C for 15 minutes until golden. Scatter with basil to serve.

## Notes
- Freezes well before baking — defrost fully and add 5 extra minutes in the oven.
